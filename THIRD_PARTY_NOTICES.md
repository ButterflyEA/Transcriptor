# Third-party notices

## Bundled model: ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3-turbo

The dictation service ships the weights of
[ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3-turbo](https://huggingface.co/ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3-turbo),
a Hebrew fine-tune by **ivrit-ai** of OpenAI's
[whisper-large-v3-turbo](https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo).

- License of the ivrit-ai model: **Apache-2.0** (per its model card).
- Base model: OpenAI Whisper, released under the MIT license.
- The model is intended for mostly-Hebrew audio. Its language detection is
  degraded, so the language is set to Hebrew by default, and it cannot translate.

Keep this notice with any redistribution of the model files.
